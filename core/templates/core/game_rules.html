Game Rules

Clear, simple rules for every Aviator round.

Know how each round works

Place your bet before the countdown ends. Once the plane takes off, cash out before it flies away to secure your result.

01

Place your bet

Bets are accepted during the betting countdown. When the round begins, the betting window closes.

02

Cash out in time

Cashout requests are checked by the server. A successful cashout before the crash is credited at the confirmed result.

03

Know the outcome

If the plane crashes while your bet is still active, that bet loses. Each round ends when the crash occurs.

Server-verified results

Bet and cashout actions are validated by the game server. You can review completed rounds on the fairness page.
